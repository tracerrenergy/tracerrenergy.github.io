Electricity settlement depends on actions and data from the transmission system operator (TSO), suppliers, and internal trading and operations teams. If the received amount differs from expectations, the cause may lie in an input, calculation, allocation, or counterparty result. The settlement manager must find where the issue began before deciding whether to correct, challenge, or accept it.

Tracerr connects the received result to expected calculations, checks, and source data, so an investigation starts with the difference rather than a hunt across files and systems.

Tracerr helps you validate that a fix has taken effect in subsequent reconciliation runs, from R1 through RF. Compare each new result with the earlier settlement and trace any remaining difference.
