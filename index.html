Radically transparent settlement for flexible energy markets.

Tracerr is a flexible framework for governing time series data and tracing its lineage. It gives companies a foundation for building transparent settlement platforms. We’ve already built ready-to-use digital twins of Elexon’s and NESO’s settlement platforms on it. With them, model expected revenue in real time or in simulations, trace differences as official runs arrive, and follow each resolution through reconciliation runs months later.

Tracerr helps you build correct trading assumptions, model secondary effects such as supplier corrections and fees, monitor settlement streams, and pinpoint where they break down.
